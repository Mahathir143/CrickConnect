Operator & User Operations Guide

CrickConnect: End-to-End User & Broadcaster Manual

Operational instructions for OBS Studio setup, CricScorer-inspired management controller, TV channel branding, and multi-device live streaming.

1. OBS Studio & Prism Live Studio Integration

Follow these steps to integrate the real-time CrickConnect scoreboard overlay into your broadcast software:

1 Add Browser Source in OBS

In OBS Studio, open your Scene. In the Sources dock, click + (Add) and select Browser. Name it CrickConnect_Overlay.

2 Enter Transparent Overlay URL & Settings

Enter your live Vercel overlay URL or local URL:

https://your-app.vercel.app/?mode=overlay
  • Width: 1920 (or 1080 for vertical 9:16 mobile live streams)
  • Height: 1080 (or 1920 for vertical streams)
  • FPS: 60
  • Custom CSS: Leave blank (the app automatically sets 100% transparent alpha everywhere).
  • Shutdown source when not visible: Disabled (unchecked) to ensure instantaneous live re-sync.

100% Alpha Channel Transparency: The overlay background is completely transparent. Only the docked bottom scorebar, top-right TV channel logo, and active center popups or celebratory stingers appear on screen.

2. CricScorer Management Controller Guide

Inspired by the popular CricScorer professional broadcast controller, the desk is organized into clear operational tiers:

Match Header & Chase Equation

Displays live scores for Batters, Bowler, Score, Boundaries (Fours & Sixes), Target, and exact chase formula (e.g. NEED 112 RUNS FROM 83 BALLS).

Circular Ball Grid & Checkbox Extras

Circular buttons: (0), (1), (2), (3), 1D, (4), (5), (6), (?). Checkboxes for Wide, No Ball, Byes, Leg Byes, and Wicket.

Wicket Fall Dropdown (8 Dismissal Modes)

Bowled, Catch out (ICC 2022 strike rules), Run out at striker end, Run out at non-striker end, Stumping, LBW, Hit wicket, and Mankad (ball does not count).

Emergency Match Adjustments

Direct override inputs with SAVE buttons to correct Inning 1 Runs, Wickets, Overs (balls), and Inning 2 figures on the fly.

3. Broadcast Layout Rules: Scoreboard & Popups

Showing or Removing the Bottom Scoreboard

Click the Bottom Scoreboard: ENABLED / REMOVED toggle button in the Score Controller bar.

  • If ENABLED: The bottom scorebar remains visible at all times, even when popups, cards, or animations appear on screen.
  • If REMOVED: The bottom scoreboard is completely hidden from the broadcast feed.

Mutual Exclusivity for Popup Cards

When you click B1, B2, BOWLER, TARGET, SUMMARY, FOW, PARTNERSHIP, or Display Input (Custom Banner):

  • Only the chosen popup is displayed in the center/lower third of the screen.
  • All other popups are automatically hidden to prevent broadcast clutter.
  • The bottom scoreboard remains visible (if enabled).
  • Click DEFAULT or STOP at any time to close all popups.

Top-Right TV Channel Logo

Click Edit Channel Name in the controller to customize your station name (e.g. CRICKCONNECT TV) and subtext (LIVE HD). It displays in the top-right corner with a live pulsing red beacon.
